Privacy and limitations

ECG files are processed in browser memory. This page does not submit ECG data to a server. Loading Pyodide requires access to a third-party CDN. The detector is a simplified, unvalidated Pan–Tompkins-style implementation; filtering coefficients are not dynamically adapted to sampling rate. RR-based rate and irregularity flags are not diagnoses of arrhythmia, and HRV from unreviewed RR intervals should not be interpreted as normal-to-normal interval HRV. Not for medical or emergency use.
